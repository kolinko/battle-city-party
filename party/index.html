In the menu: Start on a gamepad (or Enter) launches the selected mode, d-pad ↑↓ changes the selection. In a game: Start pauses, Select returns to the menu after a round ends, Esc always returns to the menu.

Controllers

Pair a gamepad with your computer over Bluetooth or USB (DualShock/DualSense: hold Share + PS until the light flashes), then press any button: the pad takes the first free slot. Browsers only see a gamepad after its first button press. The keyboard always works alongside.
